Garlic pasta

Original recipe by Allrecipes. Link

Description

This creamy pasta recipe is easy to make (requiring just one pot) and modify according to your preferences.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons of olive oil
  • 4 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 3 or more cups of chicken broth
  • ½ teaspoon of ground black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon of salt
  • ½ pound (about 230g) of spaghetti
  • 1 cup of grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¾ cup of heavy cream
  • 1 ½ tablespoons of dried parsley

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a medium pan over medium heat. Add garlic and stir for 1-2 minutes, until it's fragrant. Add butter and stir until melted.
  2. Pour in 3 cups of chicken broth. Add pepper and salt. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  3. Add spaghetti and occasionally stir until al dente (10-12 minutes). If the pasta starts sticking to the pan, add more chicken broth.
  4. Add parmesan cheese, cream, parsley. Mix thoroughly.
  5. Serve immediately.
