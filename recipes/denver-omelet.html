Denver omelet

Original recipe by Allrecipes. Link

Description

This three-egg omelet recipe has a firm texture and a strong flavour. It's easy to make and can be complemented by salad or toast among other things.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon of butter
  • ¼ cup of diced smoked ham
  • 2 tablespoons of diced onion
  • 2 tablespoons of diced green bell pepper
  • Salt
  • Ground black pepper
  • ⅓ cup of shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 1 pinch of Cayenne pepper

Steps

  1. Beat eggs in bowl until they're just about combined.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet using medium-high heat. Add diced smoked ham, onion and bell pepper. Season with salt and pepper. Cook and stir for about 5 minutes.
  3. Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour in eggs. In order to distribute the ingredients evenly, mix with a spatula while shaking the pan.
  4. Run the spatula along the edges of the omelet.
  5. Sprinkle Cheddar cheese and cayenne pepper over the omelet.
  6. Let the omelet cook for about 5 minutes, occasionally shake the pan. The top should be wet but not runny.
  7. Fold the omelet in half, transfer to a plate and serve.
